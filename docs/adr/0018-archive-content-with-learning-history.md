# Archive content with learning history

Permanent deletion is allowed only for learning content without progress history. Content that already has such history is archived, retaining its Submissions, Reviews, XP, and Mastery and their references. Archival itself does not remove XP or revoke Mastery, and the existing visibility rules continue to apply.

Archival cannot change the content of an already published coach-mode Learning Path Version. Removing content from a later draft must preserve the earlier Version and its Enrollments' learning history. This keeps evidence and progress traceable at the cost of retaining old data and requiring archive management.
