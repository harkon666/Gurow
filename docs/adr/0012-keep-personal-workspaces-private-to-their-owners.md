# Keep Personal Workspaces private to their owning Accounts

In the MVP, each Account has exactly one Personal Workspace containing all of its personal-mode Learning Paths. Only the owning Account can access this Workspace. Participating in a coach-mode Learning Path does not give that Coach access to the learner's personal Paths, Tasks, or progress.

This gives personal learning a clear privacy boundary while the same Account also participates in coached learning. Shared access and collaboration within Personal Workspaces are deferred, at the cost of users being unable to invite a Coach or another learner to directly view or work in their personal learning space during the MVP.
